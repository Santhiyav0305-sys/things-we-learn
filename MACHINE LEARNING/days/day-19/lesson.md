# Day 19 — Overfitting, underfitting and regularization

    ## Objective
    By the end of today, you should be able to explain the main idea and use it in a small program.

    ## Lesson
    Underfitting means the model is too simple to capture useful structure. Overfitting means it captures training-specific patterns that do not generalize.

Regularization constrains model complexity. Always compare training and validation performance rather than training performance alone.
