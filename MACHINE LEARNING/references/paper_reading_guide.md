# ML Paper Reading Guide

Read in this order:

1. Abstract — What problem is being solved?
2. Introduction — Why does it matter?
3. Figure/table — What is the main result?
4. Method — What is the proposed idea?
5. Experiments — What data and baselines were used?
6. Limitations — What does the paper not establish?
7. Conclusion — What should you remember?

Create a one-page summary:

- Problem:
- Previous approach:
- Proposed approach:
- Data:
- Metric:
- Baseline:
- Main result:
- Limitations:
- What I would reproduce:
